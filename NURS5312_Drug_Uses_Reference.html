NURS 5312 · Psychopharmacology · Drug Uses

What each drug is used for

A drug-to-use list grouped by therapeutic category, with each drug's class in parentheses. Many drugs cross several categories (e.g., quetiapine spans schizophrenia, bipolar, and depression).

1st-line = generally a first-line choice for its primary indication. (off-label) = widely used but not FDA-approved for that use.

Anxiety

First-line for anxiety disorders = SSRIs/SNRIs (see Antidepressants); the agents below are adjuncts or second-line.

Drug (class)Used for
Benzodiazepines (lorazepam, clonazepam, alprazolam, diazepam) (benzodiazepine)Anxiety, panic (also seizures, alcohol withdrawal, insomnia) — short-term only, not first-line
Buspirone (azapirone)Generalized anxiety disorder (GAD)
Pregabalin / Gabapentin (gabapentinoid)Neuropathic pain, fibromyalgia (off-label: GAD)
Hydroxyzine (antihistamine)Anxiety, pruritus
Propranolol (beta-blocker)Performance/somatic anxiety (off-label); also hypertension, tremor, migraine
Prazosin (alpha-blocker)PTSD nightmares (off-label); also hypertension, BPH
